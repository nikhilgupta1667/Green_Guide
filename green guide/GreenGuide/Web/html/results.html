Description: Leaf spot is a common fungal infection causing dark, round lesions on the leaves.

🏠 Home Remedies

  • Remove affected leaves immediately
  • Spray diluted neem oil every 3 days
  • Avoid overhead watering

🧪 Chemical Remedies

  • Use fungicide with chlorothalonil
  • Apply weekly as per label instructions
  • Wear gloves and avoid inhaling spray
