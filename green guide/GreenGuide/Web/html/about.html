About Green Guide

Green Guide is a passionate team of plant enthusiasts and AI experts dedicated to helping you keep your plants healthy and thriving. Using state-of-the-art artificial intelligence, we provide fast and accurate plant disease diagnosis and effective remedies to save your greenery.

How Our AI Works

Our AI analyzes the images you upload by comparing them to thousands of plant disease examples. It identifies patterns and symptoms to diagnose the condition of your plant. No technical knowledge required — just snap a clear picture and let Green Guide do the rest!

Frequently Asked Questions

❓

How do I take a good plant image?

Make sure your plant is well-lit, use natural light if possible, focus on affected areas, and avoid blurry or too distant shots.

🔒

Is my data safe?

Absolutely! We do not share your images or information with third parties. Your privacy is our priority.

Can I get help for all plant types?

Green Guide supports a wide variety of common plants. We continually update our AI to support more species.
